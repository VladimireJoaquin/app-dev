# My Favorite Anime

## Solo Leveling

**Genre:** Action, Fantasy, Adventure

**Main Character:** Sung Jinwoo

## About the Anime
Solo Leveling is one of my favorite anime. It is about Sung Jinwoo, a weak hunter who becomes stronger as he levels up.

## Favorite Characters
- Sung Jinwoo
- Cha Hae-in
- Igris

## Why I Like It
I like Solo Leveling because of its action scenes and interesting story. My favorite character is Sung Jinwoo because he is strong and cool.
